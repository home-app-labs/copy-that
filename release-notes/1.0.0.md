The first public version of Copy That.

- Clipboard history behind ⇧⌘V: a strip of cards at the bottom of the screen, on top of any app
- Previews for text, images and screenshots, and files
- Links are shown as plain cards built from the address itself. The app never contacts the network
- Search as you type, tabs for text, links, images and files
- Saved snippets in named groups, written by hand or saved from the history
- Paste with Return, as plain text with Shift-Return, or by number with ⌘1–9
- History limits by number of items and by age
- Entries that password managers mark as secret are not recorded

**Install:** download `Copy-That.pkg` and open it. macOS blocks it the first time because the app is not signed with a paid Apple developer certificate: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway". Pictures of each step are on https://homeapplabs.com/install/

Works on macOS 14 or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
